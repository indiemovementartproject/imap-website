How your money moves

Payments go straight from your UPI app to iMAP’s bank account. This site never sees or stores your UPI PIN, card or bank details — we only record your name, contact and the transaction reference so we can match your payment and confirm your seat. Email is optional — leave it out and we’ll confirm on WhatsApp.
